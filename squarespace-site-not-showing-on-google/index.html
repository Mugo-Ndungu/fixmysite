Guides: Getting found on Google

Squarespace site not showing on Google?

Squarespace keeps sites out of search results while they're on a trial, password-protected, or set to be hidden from search engines. Once the site is public and paid, connect Google Search Console so Google finds it faster.

1. Make sure the site is public

Sites on a free trial or behind a site-wide password can't be listed by Google. Check your site availability settings and make sure the site is public and on a paid plan.

2. Check the search engine setting

In your SEO settings, make sure Hide site from search engines is turned off. Then check the SEO tab of your important pages, which can also be hidden one by one.

3. Use your own domain

Connect your own domain so Google lists that, rather than the temporary squarespace.com address. If the domain shows "Website Expired", see the expired Squarespace guide.

4. Connect Google Search Console

Squarespace can connect Search Console from its SEO or analytics settings. Then submit yourbusiness.com/sitemap.xml (Squarespace creates it automatically) and request indexing for your homepage.

5. Give each page a clear title and description

In each page's SEO settings, write a title that says what you do and where, and a one-sentence description. That's what people see in Google before they click.
