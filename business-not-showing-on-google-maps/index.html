Guides: Getting found on Google

Business not showing on Google Maps?

Usually the Business Profile isn't verified, is missing key details, or has been suspended. Fixing those gets you onto the map. Ranking higher among nearby competitors then comes down to a complete profile, reviews, and a website that works.

1. Check the profile exists and is verified

Sign in to the Google account that manages the business and search for your business name. If Google asks you to verify, finish that first: unverified profiles often don't show on Maps. If you can't find a profile at all, create one at Google Business Profile.

2. Fill in every field

Use your real business name (no extra keywords), the right primary category, opening hours, phone number, website and photos. Service-area businesses such as plumbers can hide their street address and list the areas they serve instead.

3. Look for a suspension

If the profile shows as suspended, Google believes something breaks its guidelines: often a name stuffed with keywords, a virtual office address, or duplicate listings. Correct the cause, then submit the reinstatement request from the profile.

4. Make the website match

Your website should show the same business name, phone number and address as the profile. A broken website link is a common problem (see wrong website on Google Maps), and so is a site that doesn't load.

5. Ask happy customers for reviews

Recent, genuine reviews help you appear for nearby searches. Ask after a job well done and reply to every review, good or bad.
