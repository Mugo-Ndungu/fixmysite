Guides: Site down or showing an error

Your website shows a hosting company's default page or "Index of /"?

That page means your domain reaches a server, but the server has no website set up for it, or the files are missing or in the wrong folder. It often happens after a hosting change, an account reset, or a plan that lapsed.

1. Read what the page says

"Web Server's Default Page" or a hosting company's logo means the server has no site for your domain. "Index of /" means the folder exists but has no homepage file. A "parked" or "for sale" page usually means a domain problem: see the expired domain guide.

2. Check where your domain points

If you recently moved hosts or builders, the domain may point to the wrong server. See the DNS guide to check your records.

3. Check the hosting account

Log in to your hosting panel. Is the plan active? Is your domain added to the account? Are your website files in the main web folder (often public_html)? If the folder is empty, the files may have been deleted or the account reset.

4. Restore the website

Most hosts keep backups for a limited time. Restore the most recent one from the backups section, or ask support. If there's no backup, the site has to be rebuilt.

5. Keep your own backup

Download a copy of your site every few months, or use a backup service, so the business never depends on one host's backups.
