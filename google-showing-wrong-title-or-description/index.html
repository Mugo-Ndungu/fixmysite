Guides: Getting found on Google

Google shows the wrong title or description for your website?

Google builds your search result from your page's title and description tags, and from text on the page. If those are missing, out of date or generic (like "Home"), Google shows whatever it finds. Set them properly, then ask Google to recrawl.

1. Check what your page says now

Open your homepage, view the page source, and look for <title> and <meta name="description">. A missing description, an old business name or just "Home" explains what Google shows.

2. Write a better title

Say what you do and where, under about 60 characters, for example "Emergency Plumber in Lakeland, FL | Smith Plumbing". Give every page its own title.

3. Write a description

One or two sentences, under about 155 characters, saying what you offer and why someone should click. Google may still rewrite it, but a good one is used far more often.

4. Where to change them

  • WordPress: your SEO plugin's box under each page (Yoast, Rank Math), or the theme settings.
  • Wix and Squarespace: each page's SEO settings (see the Wix and Squarespace guides).
  • Shopify: Online Store, Preferences for the homepage, and each product's search engine listing.

5. Ask Google to update

In Search Console, use URL Inspection on the page and click Request indexing. Updates usually show within days to a couple of weeks. If your business name changed, update your Business Profile too.
