Website not showing on Google?

Most often a setting is telling Google to stay away: a "noindex" tag, a robots.txt block, or a "hide from search engines" option in your site builder. Remove it, then ask Google to look again in Search Console. Pages usually come back within days to a few weeks.

1. Check whether Google has any of your pages

Search Google for site:yourbusiness.com (with your own address). No results means Google has none of your pages. A few results but not the ones you want means individual pages are blocked.

2. Look for a "noindex" tag

Open your homepage, right-click and choose View page source, then search the page (Ctrl+F or Cmd+F) for noindex. If you find <meta name="robots" content="noindex">, that line is telling Google not to list the page.

Where it usually comes from:

  • WordPress: Settings, then Reading. Untick "Discourage search engines from indexing this site". SEO plugins such as Yoast or Rank Math can also set noindex on single pages.
  • Wix: in your dashboard's SEO settings, make sure "Let search engines index your site" is switched on. Check each page's SEO settings too.
  • Squarespace: in the SEO settings, turn off "Hide site from search engines", and check the SEO tab of each page.
  • Shopify: a store still behind its password page can't be listed. Remove the password once the store is ready.

3. Check robots.txt

Visit yourbusiness.com/robots.txt. If you see User-agent: * followed by Disallow: /, the whole site is blocked. That line is often left behind from when the site was being built. Remove it, or ask whoever built the site to.

4. Ask Google to come back

Add your site to Google Search Console (free), open URL Inspection, paste your homepage address and click Request indexing. If your site has a sitemap, submit it under Sitemaps.
