Website contact form not sending emails?

Usually the messages are being sent but not delivered: they land in spam, go to an old email address, or get blocked because the website sends mail in a way inboxes don't trust. A proper sending setup (SMTP) fixes most WordPress cases.

1. Send yourself a test

Fill in the form yourself, then check your inbox and spam folder. Check the spam folder of every address the form might send to.

2. Check where the form sends

Open the form's settings and look at the notification or recipient email. It's often still set to the web designer's address, or an old staff mailbox.

3. WordPress: send mail properly

By default WordPress sends email straight from the web server, and many inboxes treat that as spam or reject it. Install an SMTP plugin such as WP Mail SMTP and connect it to your real email account (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, or your email provider). Then send a test from the plugin's settings.

4. Wix and Squarespace

Check the form's settings for the address notifications go to, and look in the platform's own inbox or form submissions area: messages are usually stored there even when the email doesn't arrive.

5. Fix your domain's email records

If you send from an address on your own domain, your domain needs SPF and DKIM records so inboxes trust its mail. Your email provider lists the exact records to add at your domain company.
