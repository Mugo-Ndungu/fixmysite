Wix site not showing on Google?

Usually one of three things: the site is set to stay out of search results, the pages you care about are individually hidden, or Google simply hasn't been told the site exists yet. Wix has settings for all three, and fixing them takes minutes. Google then needs days to a few weeks.

1. Check the site-wide setting

In your Wix dashboard, open the SEO settings and make sure Let search engines index your site is switched on. If it's off, nothing on the site can appear in Google.

2. Check individual pages

In the Wix editor, open each important page's SEO settings (Pages, then the page's settings, then SEO basics). Make sure it isn't set to be hidden from search results. Pages copied from a template are sometimes hidden by default.

3. Make sure people can reach the site on your own domain

Google lists the address it can actually reach. If your domain shows "This domain isn't connected to a site", fix that first (see the Wix domain guide). Also make sure the site is published.

4. Tell Google about it

Wix's SEO setup checklist can connect your site to Google Search Console for you. Once connected, use URL Inspection on your homepage and click Request indexing. Wix creates your sitemap automatically at yourbusiness.com/sitemap.xml, so submit that too.

5. Give each page a clear title

A page called "Home" with no description gives Google nothing to match. In each page's SEO settings, use a title that says what you do and where, for example "Plumber in Lakeland, FL | Smith Plumbing".
