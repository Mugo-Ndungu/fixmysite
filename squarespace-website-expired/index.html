Squarespace says "Website Expired"?

Squarespace shows this page when the website's subscription has ended, most often because a card payment failed. The owner can log in and resubscribe to bring the site back. Don't wait: expired sites are only kept for a limited time.

1. Log in as the owner

Click Owner login on the expired page, or go to squarespace.com and log in with the email address the site was set up with.

2. Renew the subscription

Open the site's billing settings, update the card if needed, and choose a plan. The site comes back as it was once the subscription is active.

3. If you can't get into the account

Use Squarespace's password reset with the original email. If that email address no longer works (common when a former employee or web designer set the site up), contact Squarespace support and ask them to help you prove ownership.

4. Check your domain too

If your domain was bought through Squarespace, it renews separately from the website. Make sure it hasn't expired as well, or your address may stop working even after the site is back.

5. Stop it happening again

Use a card that won't expire soon, and make sure billing emails go to an inbox someone actually reads.
