Shopify store stuck on the "Opening soon" password page?

Every new Shopify store starts behind a password page. You remove it in your Online Store preferences, but Shopify only lets you do that once you've chosen a paid plan. While the password is on, customers can't buy and Google can't list your products.

1. Choose a plan

If you're still on a free trial, Shopify won't let you remove the password. In your Shopify admin, go to Settings, then Plan, and choose a plan.

2. Turn off password protection

In your Shopify admin, open Online Store, then Preferences. Find Password protection, untick the box that restricts access to visitors with the password, and save.

3. Check it from outside

Open your store in a private browser window or on your phone. You should see your homepage, not the "Opening soon" page.

4. Check the basics before customers arrive

  • Your domain is connected and loads with the padlock (https).
  • Payments and shipping rates are set up, and you've placed a test order.
  • Your store's title and description in the Preferences page say what you sell.

5. Tell Google

Shopify creates your sitemap at yourstore.com/sitemap.xml. Add the store to Google Search Console and submit it, so your products start appearing in search.
