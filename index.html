Questions

If yours isn't here, just ask.

Do you need my password?

No. Wix, Squarespace, WordPress, Shopify, Google Business Profile and domain registrars all let you add me as a user or collaborator. You can remove me as soon as the job is done.

What if you can't fix it?

Then you don't pay. If I find the problem is bigger than we agreed, I'll tell you before doing any more work, with a new fixed price you can accept or decline.

How do I pay?

By card or PayPal through an invoice I send after the fix. You can pay in US dollars, pounds or euros.

You're in Kenya and I'm in the US / UK. Does that work?

Yes. Everything is done online, and most fixes don't need a call. I work late afternoons and evenings Nairobi time so I overlap with US business hours, and UK and Irish mornings.

Which platforms do you work with?

WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, GoDaddy and Hostinger builders, plain HTML sites, and custom sites built with React, Next.js, Node.js or PHP.

Can you keep an eye on it after?

Yes, with optional monthly care: updates, backups, and alerts if your site goes down or your certificate is about to expire. Cancel any time.
