Guides: Domains, DNS and email

Wix says "This domain isn't connected to a site"?

Wix shows this page when your domain reaches Wix but isn't attached to a published site. The usual causes are a Premium plan that lapsed, a site that was unpublished, or DNS settings that changed at your domain company.

1. Check the domain in your Wix account

Log in to Wix and open the Domains page from your account settings. It shows each domain, which site it's attached to, and whether anything needs fixing.

2. Check your Premium plan

Connecting your own domain needs an active Wix Premium plan. If a card payment failed, the plan may have ended and the domain disconnected with it. Renew the plan and reconnect the domain.

3. Make sure the site is published

Open the site in the Wix editor and click Publish. An unpublished site can't show on your domain.

4. If you bought the domain somewhere else

If your domain is with GoDaddy, Namecheap or another company, Wix shows the exact records to set there (either Wix's name servers or pointing records). Enter them at your domain company. Changes can take up to 48 hours to work everywhere.

5. If you've moved to a new website

Seeing this after switching away from Wix means the domain still points at Wix. Change the DNS records at your domain company to point to your new host instead.
