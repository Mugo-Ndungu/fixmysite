Guides: Getting found on Google

Google Maps shows the wrong website for your business?

The "Website" button comes from your Google Business Profile. If it points to an old address, an expired domain or an unrelated site, change it in the profile. If you don't manage the profile yet, claim it first.

1. Open your Business Profile

Sign in to the Google account that manages your business, then search Google for your business name. Your profile appears with an Edit profile option.

2. Change the website link

Go to Edit profile, then Contact, and update Website to your correct address, starting with https://. Save. Google may review the change, so allow a few days.

3. If you don't manage the profile

On your Google Maps listing, look for Own this business? or Claim this business and follow the steps. Google verifies you by phone, email, video or postcard depending on the business.

4. Check the other links too

Menu, booking and appointment links can point to old pages as well. Check them while you're in the profile.

5. If the wrong link keeps coming back

Anyone can suggest edits to a listing. If yours keeps changing to someone else's site, keep the profile verified, review suggested edits, and report it to Google Business Profile support.
